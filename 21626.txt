Ticket - Computer won't power on

Jot down process you would go through if you got this ticket and had to go over to the person's desk and try to troubleshoot the problem

Steps
Why you would take those steps

My steps
1) First check is the cable connected to the outlet and to the computer?
If not, plug it in, and otherwise try another cable
To ensure that the hardware layer is connected 
2) Check the PSU and ensure everything is working 
To ensure the hardware layer doesn't have something faulty
Make sure everything is screwed in properly 
Otherwise reinstall a new PSU
3) In the meantime provided another computer for the employee to work in while I attempt to troubleshoot

General strategies for troubleshooting hardware issues
1) Have a good understanding inside a system and their components
Monitor component - cable to monitor - cable to computer - graphics on the MOBO
Think of all the different problem areas
2) Break down components to lowest testable parts and isolating the part
3) Order in which to do testing
Try the easiest thing first

Check power cable, check power strip, check PSU power button is ON 
Verify outlet or power bar are working properly plug in a known device to test 
Open computer case to verify connections from power button to MOBO, try unplugging and replugging them. Consult manual to verify the power ON pins and try to manually jumper them
Sometimes the power button breaks after pushing too many times
Check the CMOS battery and reset CMOS either by unplugging battery or with the jumpers
Check PSU connections to MOBO are okay, inspect PSU for any visible signs of damage (scorched or black)
Test PSU or replace with known functioning PSU
Test MOBO then replace with known good MOBO
