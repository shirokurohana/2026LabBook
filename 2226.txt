Intro to IT
- Definition
- What does IT do?

What is IT?
- Actual roles in a modern business
- IT = Information Technology
  - Broad term 
  - All tech work at a company
  - For example software dev or engineering

- Focus on more traditional IT definition
  - Companies who don't deliver tech products use this more traditional IT definition
  - Responsible administering, deploying, supporting technology that the organization uses to support business functions
  - Excludes software development and engineering

- What does role IT dept play?
  - Manages and supports technology required for the organization to function
- IT departments play an integral role in modern businesses
  - Crucial to smooth operation and growth of the business
  - May be outsourced - MSP - Managed Service Provider
- IT department responsibilities can vary between organizations
  - Depends on size 

IT Department Responsibilities
- Infrastructure Management
  - Oversee installation and maintenance and upgrades of technological assets
  - Computers, phones, even cloud 
- Tech support
  - Support employees to effectively use tech within the company
  - Can include troubleshooting, training, setup and administration tasks (password resets)
- Security
  - Sometimes it's own department or sub dept
  - Frequently includes both cybersecurity and physical security (security cameras and checking locks on doors)
- Data Management
  - Manage the organization's data and data systems
  - Access controls and auditing for data (permissions to view certain data)
  - Database administration
- Identity and Access Management
  - Login accounts
  - Creating, administering, and auditing accounts
  - Onboarding and offboarding employees
- Project Management
  - Planning and managing IT projects
  - Includes upgrades or transitions to new solutions
  - Includes strategic planning to keep business ready for new demands
- Compliance and Governance
  - Ensure organziation adheres to standards
  - Privacy and protection

IT dept is responsible for deploying, administering, and supporting tech used by a company in biz operations.
