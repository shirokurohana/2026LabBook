Help Desk Roles and Responsibilities

Individual contributor responsible for providing technical support and assistance to end-users, either within an organization or to customers

- Front line technical support to end users
- Troubleshoot hardware, software, network, and other issues
- Password and account management, such as password resets
- Customer service
- Escalation of support to appropriate people or channels
- Fixing or installing hardware
- Installing or configuring software
- Maintain and create documentation

Face of IT dept
Level 1 - frontline - must know when to escalate and document well

Help desk is first point of contact for users when technical assistance is needed
Support is usually assigned via a ticketing system
Support by provided or the phone, remotely or in person

Troubleshooting 
- Help desk is frequently presented with problems a user encountered that they must troubleshoot 
- This can range from hardware, software, networking or other msic items
- Part of the responsibility to gather info to properly troubleshoot
- Look at prior documentation

Password and account management
- Assist users with password resets or unlocking accounts
- Create new accounts or modify existing ones
- Follow guidelines to ensure security

Customer service
- Provide quality customer service
- Help desk can be the face of the IT dept or even organization
- Frequently users will be dealing with stressful situations
- Soft skills are integral to developping good relationships and building trust with end users

Escalation of support
- Help desk tiered L1-L3
- Important to know the limitations of each role and when/where to escalate
- May be internal documentation such as guides or runbooks that dictate escalation levels

Fixing or installing hardware
- Some help desk roles require troubleshooting, fixing or installing of hardware such as computers
- Even in roles that are remote or don't require hands-on work with hardware, knowledge of hardware issues is important

Install or configure software
- Help desk installs basic approved software or tools
- Frequently end users will not have rights or permissions to install
- May require initial training of end user on how to use the software

Maintain and create documentation
- Help desk usually relies on internal documentation guides, runbooks, playbooks, troubleshooting documentation
- Documenation is usually created by help desk team
- New tasks help desk will be tasked with updating documenation
